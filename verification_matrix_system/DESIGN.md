## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, high-precision verification aesthetic engineered for enterprise compliance and environmental accreditation. The interface operates as an avant-garde audit terminal: deliberate, analytical, and authoritative. 

Rather than leaning into generic corporate softness, the system draws from a synthesis of **Structural Brutalism** and **Technical Editorialism**. It strips away arbitrary decoration in favor of visible mechanical scaffolding, razor-sharp geometric panels, monospaced data alignment, and unyielding zero-radius structures.

The target audience consists of lead auditors, sustainability directors, and compliance officers navigating rigorous regulatory frameworks. The emotional response evoked is absolute structural certainty, uncompromised transparency, and cryptographic rigor. Environmental metrics are treated not as decorative marketing tokens, but as forensic evidentiary standards where every data point occupies an immutable structural cell.

## Colors

The color architecture relies on a surgical, high-luminance background contrasted against deep structural dark-slate inks and high-frequency signal accents.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#10B981`):** The definitive verification accent. Utilized strictly for confirmed environmental thresholds, certified compliance gates, cryptographic validation markers, and finalized positive states. A deeper tone (`#059669`) provides foreground legibility on white surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#0B132B`):** Deep abyssal navy. Deployed as structural boundaries, high-density data headers, terminal frames, and primary interactive ink.
- **Tertiary (`#3B82F6`):** Precision instrument blue. Reserved for currently active workflow phases, live cursor states, selected checklist items, and focus loops.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Obsidian black/slate. Delivers uncompromising typographic contrast across all diagnostic metrics.

### Status Signals & Diagnostics
- **Surveillance / Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Monitored non-conformity risks, pending re-audits, expiring certification dates, and attention-requiring gates.
- **Critical Deviation / Danger (`#EF4444`):** Hard compliance breaches, failed criteria, expired permits, and audit rejection states.

### Surfaces & Division
- Backgrounds remain predominantly stark (`#F8FAFC`), while interactive cards and data partitions rest on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or technical offset tints (`#F1F5F9`).
- Borders act as functional spatial gridlines via hairline slate (`#CBD5E1`) and high-impact structural dividers (`#0F172A`).

## Typography

The typographic pairing merges technical mechanical precision with rapid editorial legibility.

- **Space Grotesk** governs structural titles, audit IDs, criteria counters, and technical labels. Its geometric engineering conveys systemic control, transforming standard page headers into institutional ledger entries. All metadata tags and micro-labels leverage uppercase tracking to reinforce tabular structure.
- **Hanken Grotesk** serves as the utilitarian workhorse for dense descriptive criteria, auditor commentary, and compliance statements. It delivers neutral contrast and clear eye-tracking through multi-column verification matrices without visual exhaustion.

All metrics, audit clock intervals, and status tags are vertically aligned along strict baseline rhythms to guarantee dense, structured balance across deep nested tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is founded on an asymmetric, dense technical matrix. 

### Grid Dynamics
- Desktop environments leverage a strict 12-column structural grid system anchored by a permanent contextual terminal sidebar (span 3 or 4) and a modular verification cockpit (span 8 or 9).
- Gutters remain tight (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to preserve semantic proximity between audit clauses and compliance verification controls.
- On smaller viewports, the grid collapses linearly into single-column diagnostic sheets, while critical contextual action bars lock directly to top and bottom screen perimeters.

### Spacing Discipline
Spacing conforms to an uncompromising 4px metric rhythm. Dense operational panels minimize decorative margins, prioritizing data density and rapid visual parsing over loose negative space. Component interior padding uses tight boundaries (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure that expansive compliance frameworks display complete verification gates within the immediate viewport frame.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects ambient, diffused drop shadows. Spatial hierarchy is communicated exclusively through **Structural Tonal Layering** and **High-Definition Outlines**.

### Structural Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground-level substrate (`#F8FAFC`), representing non-interactive background space.
2. **Level 1 (Operational Matrix):** Audit cards, document trays, and checklist modules in sharp white (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by 1px hairline borders (`#CBD5E1`).
3. **Level 2 (Active Inspection):** Selected criteria panels, focused validation fields, and active terminal steps bordered by 1.5px solid midnight slate (`#0B132B`) or instrument blue (`#3B82F6`).
4. **Level 3 (Fixed Overlays & Heads-Up Action Bars):** Persistent audit action bars and modal audit sheets utilize pure solid fills with distinct 2px borders, accompanied by an optional solid, zero-blur structural offset edge (`3px 3px 0px #0B132B`) for terminal popovers and verification badges.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly zero-radius (`roundedness: 0`). 

No rounded corners, soft bubbles, or circular avatars exist within the system. Every button, data pill, card, input cell, and indicator badge is cut with absolute orthogonal 90-degree corners. 

This strict geometric discipline evokes architectural blueprints, printed technical compliance sheets, and ledger apparatuses. Interactive elements rely on sharp perimeter divisions, internal grid lines, and high-contrast color blocks to communicate interactability and state boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Audit Action:** Solid obsidian navy (`#0B132B`) with crisp white typography in uppercase `Space Grotesk`. 0px radius. Hover shifts to Emerald Green (`#10B981`) with instant color swap (0ms ease).
- **Conformity Action:** Solid emerald green (`#10B981`) fill, black text (`#0B132B`), explicitly used for confirming criteria compliance or approving steps.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#0B132B` border. Hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Verification Tags
- Rectangular zero-radius data chips with strict 1px borders.
- **Passed Gate:** Green background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#065F46`), 1px solid `#10B981` border. Accompanied by a leading bracket `[OK]`.
- **Surveillance / Warning:** Amber background (`#FFFBEB`), text (`#92400E`), 1px solid `#F59E0B` border. Bracket prefix `[WARN]`.
- **Overdue / Breach:** Red background (`#FEF2F2`), text (`#991B1B`), 1px solid `#EF4444` border. Bracket prefix `[FAIL]`.

### Checklists & Compliance Steppers
- **Collapsible Checklist Modules:** Accordion units separated by sharp horizontal hairline dividers. Headers feature criteria codes (e.g., `GK-7.2.1`), status chips, and direct compliance toggles.
- **Verification Gates:** Binary and ternary switches designed as segmented zero-radius bar selectors: `[NON-COMPLIANT]`, `[IN PROGRESS]`, `[VERIFIED]`. Active states fully invert color.

### Form Inputs & Terminal Inspection Cells
- Unrounded input boxes with persistent 1px borders in `#CBD5E1`.
- Focused state shifts border to 2px solid `#3B82F6` with zero glow.
- Monospaced metric inputs (e.g., CO2 calculations, energy audits) feature embedded units in right-aligned technical labels.

### Context-Aware Action Bars
- Pinned bottom docking strip spanning 100% width or fixed cleanly to the audit workspace container.
- High-contrast navy slate container (`#0B132B`) featuring live tally metrics (e.g., `TOTAL CRITERIA: 84`, `CONFORMITY: 92%`, `CRITICAL FLAGS: 0`) alongside direct validation triggers (`REJECT BATCH`, `GENERATE CERTIFICATE`).

### Audit Clocks & Timers
- Sharp boxed chronometers with monospaced tracking.
- Expiring verification deadlines flash with high-contrast amber or red borders, showing remaining hours and minutes in an unadorned structural cell.
